About me

Hi! My name is Erik and I have being playing and painting Warhammer miniatures for more than 30 years.

I found the game at the not that tender age of 12 yeras and soon I got in love with the epic pictures of the magazine White Dwarf. As soon as I saved enough money I bought my first warhammer box: the fifth edition Warhammer fantasy starting box that included lizardmen and bretonnian armies. Since then have I gathered an unhealthy amount of plastic and tin soldiers.

In my humble origins I did many many mistakes, and nowadays i keep doing them. That is why many are the thechniques that I have learned, like these bellow:

Freehand

Do you prefer painting 2D areas or 3D models? Why not both? Don't limit yourself to the use of decals.

Airbrushing

There is a before and an after anyone gets an airbrush. It completelly changes the way you prime, make gradients, put lights (both zenithal and OLS), varnish, and several other tricks.

OSL

Object Source Light is about reflecting the light from an intense source that is not the zenithal sun light.

Slap chop

The perfect technique for its purpose: to get many miniatures painted as fast as posible. Black base, quick drybrush to create the light intensity, followed by transparent colours than tint instead of cover.

Warhammer Fantasy

My first big strategy game was Warhammer Fantasy. For years it was my main game, untill, unfortunatelly, it was discontinued.

Bretonnia

The return of warhammer fantasy as The Old World included a bretonnian army as a start army. I could just not resist to buy and build an army of the chivarlrous faction.
